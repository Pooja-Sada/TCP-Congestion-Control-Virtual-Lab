How TCP Recovery Works

Fast Recovery

After receiving three duplicate ACKs, TCP performs Fast Retransmit, sets ssthresh to half the previous congestion window, and temporarily inflates cwnd by 3 MSS. When a new ACK arrives, cwnd returns to ssthresh and TCP enters Congestion Avoidance.

Timeout

When a retransmission timeout occurs, TCP reduces ssthresh to half the previous congestion window, resets cwnd to 1 MSS, and returns to Slow Start.
